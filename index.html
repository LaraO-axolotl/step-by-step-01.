Managing Problems

A step-by-step worksheet adapted from the WHO "Managing Problems" strategy (PM+ manual, ch. 7). It helps you sort your problems, choose one, and make a small plan. Nothing leaves your browser.

Self-reflection tool, not therapy or medical advice. If you feel hopeless, very low or unsafe, please talk to a health professional or someone you trust. Adapted from: WHO (2018), Problem Management Plus (PM+), Chapter 7.
